# Northstar v8.7.4 — Release Notes

**Released:** 2026-07-01

---

## What's New

### Added

- The **Retirement Readiness** tool now includes a **Can I Retire Now?** tab, designed as an India FIRE calculator. It lets you test whether your current corpus can sustain retirement by modelling inflation, returns, and living expenses in one place.
- The Can I Retire Now? tab includes combination risk scenarios and an insurance and risk instruments section. These additions let you stress-test your retirement plan against multiple risks occurring together and review how insurance factors into your safety margin.
- A new **Setup tab** has been added to consolidate configuration options that were previously tucked away in the sidebar. Your key inputs are now easier to find and edit from a single dedicated place.
- The **FI Ladder** feature has been introduced, along with a Plan Breaker analysis and enhanced AI insights. These tools help you visualise your path to financial independence and identify the assumptions most likely to derail your plan.
- The **Tail Risk Dashboard** is now available as a dedicated tab. It surfaces tail-risk information in one focused view so you can assess low-probability, high-impact scenarios for your portfolio.
- The Housing and Cashflow tab has been rewritten with updated inputs and layout. This makes it easier to enter your housing situation, EMI details, and cashflow assumptions in a structured way.
- The sidebar now includes insurance audit fields and health expense inputs. These let you account for insurance coverage and anticipated healthcare costs when modelling your financial position.
- The scenario comparison table in Retirement Readiness now includes **corpus at time of purchase** and **minimum safe corpus** columns. These additional columns make it easier to compare scenarios and judge how close each one comes to your safety threshold.
- Fund search in **Update Price for One Fund** now accepts a fund name, ISIN, or AMFI code. You can locate the fund you want to update using whichever identifier is most convenient.

### Changed

- The tool formerly called **Crash Simulator** has been renamed to **Retirement Readiness**. The name better reflects what the tool actually helps you do.

### Fixed

- The Can I Retire Now? tab now correctly reads default inflation and return values from your sidebar settings. Previously it ignored those inputs, which could lead to mismatched assumptions.
- The Can I Retire Now? tab was using a figure twelve times larger than intended as the default annual expenses. Expenses now default to the correct annual amount.
- The FI Ladder chart's y-axis now displays values in Crores (₹X.XX Cr) and uses a clean set of 4–6 tick intervals. Charts were previously showing raw numbers that were harder to read at the scales typical for retirement planning.
- An incorrect calculation in the Tail Risk tab that summed individual risk gaps has been removed. This prevents a misleading total risk figure from appearing in your results.
- The housing sidebar now correctly extracts the EMI value from the calculation result. Previously a formatting error could cause the EMI field to display incorrectly.
- Goal mappings now refresh correctly when a debt entry is deleted. Previously, stale data could persist and affect goal-related calculations after a deletion.

## How to Upgrade

**Use `upgrade.py` — never extract the zip manually.**
It preserves your `.env` (database credentials, license key, API key) and runs
any new database migrations automatically. Your portfolio data is untouched.

1. Download your platform zip anywhere (e.g. your Downloads folder):
   - **Windows:** `pages_app_v8.7.4_windows.zip`
   - **Mac:** `pages_app_v8.7.4_mac.zip`
2. Open a terminal:
   - **Windows:** press `Win + R`, type `cmd`, press Enter
   - **Mac:** press `Cmd + Space`, type `Terminal`, press Enter
3. Run the upgrade command (replace the paths with your actual locations):

   **Windows:**
   ```
   python C:\pages_app\upgrade.py C:\Users\You\Downloads\pages_app_v8.7.4_windows.zip
   ```
   **Mac:**
   ```
   python ~/pages_app/upgrade.py ~/Downloads/pages_app_v8.7.4_mac.zip
   ```
4. Restart the app when it prints **"Upgrade complete."**

> **Tip:** Keep a copy of your `.env` file somewhere safe as a general backup.
> If you ever need to reinstall from scratch, it has everything you need.

> **First install?** Follow `GETTING_STARTED.md` from the top.
